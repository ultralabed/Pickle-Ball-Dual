What is Pickle Ball Dual?

Pickle Ball Dual is a free 3D pickleball game that runs in your web browser. You play singles or doubles against computer-controlled opponents at DUPR levels 3.0 to 6.0, using the real rules of pickleball, with no download and no sign-up.

Can I play pickleball online for free?

Yes. Pickle Ball Dual is free to play online in your browser: open the page, press Play and start a match, with no download, account or payment. You play against AI opponents, because there is no online multiplayer.

Does Pickle Ball Dual need a download, plug-in or account?

No. It runs as a web page and needs only a recent desktop browser with WebGL and a keyboard. Your settings and last match setup are saved in your own browser, not on a server.

How do I play on a Mac?

Open Pickle Ball Dual in Safari or Chrome on your Mac, press Play and use the keyboard: W, A, S, D to move, Space to drive, and J, K and L for the drop, dink and lob. If the court does not appear, turn on hardware acceleration in your browser, and choose Low graphics on an older Mac.

Which browsers and devices does it work on?

Pickle Ball Dual is built for desktop browsers such as recent Chrome and Safari, with WebGL and a keyboard. It has no touch controls, so it is not playable on a phone or tablet without a keyboard.

Can I play with friends or against other people online?

No. Pickle Ball Dual is single-player: in doubles you and an AI partner face two AI opponents, and in singles you face one. It has no online multiplayer and no accounts.

What is the two-bounce rule in pickleball?

The two-bounce rule says the serve must bounce on the receiving side and the return must bounce on the serving side before anyone may volley. After those two bounces, players can hit the ball out of the air, except in the kitchen.

What does 0-0-2 mean in pickleball?

0-0-2 is the opening score call in doubles: the serving side has 0, the receiving side has 0, and the server is server number 2. The first serving side gets only one server in its first turn, so it begins as server 2 and loses the serve after its first lost rally.

What is the kitchen in pickleball?

The kitchen, or non-volley zone, is the 7 ft (2.13 m) area on each side of the net where you may not hit the ball out of the air. You may stand in it, and you may hit a ball that has bounced there. Its lines count as part of the kitchen.

What is a dink in pickleball?

A dink is a soft, low shot that lands in the opponent's kitchen so they cannot attack it. Players trade dinks from the kitchen line until someone pops up a high ball to smash. In the game you dink with the K key.

What is an ATP shot in pickleball?

An ATP, or around-the-post shot, is hit around the outside of the net post instead of over the net, so it may pass below net height. It is legal if it lands in the opponent's court. In Pickle Ball Dual it happens automatically when you reach a wide ball near the net.

What is DUPR?

DUPR stands for Dynamic Universal Pickleball Rating, a skill rating for pickleball players that runs from about 2.0 to 8.0. Pickle Ball Dual uses four levels (3.0, 4.0, 5.0 and 6.0) to set how strong the AI plays; it is not an official rating and does not change your real DUPR.

Which DUPR level should I choose?

Start at 3.0 to learn the controls: that AI is slow and makes many errors. Move up to 4.0 when you win comfortably, and try 5.0 and 6.0 when you want fast, patient opponents that attack high balls and rarely make errors.

What is the difference between traditional and rally scoring?

In traditional (side-out) scoring only the serving side can score and games go to 11, win by 2, with two servers per turn in doubles. In rally scoring every rally gives a point to its winner and the game here goes to 15, win by 2.

Does Pickle Ball Dual follow the real pickleball rules?

Mostly. It implements the diagonal serve, the two-bounce rule, the kitchen, the common faults, side-out and rally scoring and the 0-0-2 start. It simplifies a few details: kitchen momentum is a 0.6 second window, and it has no end changes, time-outs or net-touch faults.

How do I know when to hit the ball?

Watch the ring under your player's feet: amber means the ball is coming, green means press a hit key now, lime is perfect timing, and red means swinging now would be a fault. The paddle meets the ball about 0.17 seconds after you press.

Can I play Pickle Ball Dual in Chinese?

Yes. The game and these guides are available in English and Traditional Chinese (繁體中文). Switch language from the main menu or Settings. The referee voice is always English.
